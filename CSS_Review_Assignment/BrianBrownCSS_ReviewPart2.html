I am a resident of the state of Florida. While many people have asked me how I can live there with the hurricanes, I ask them about their earthquakes, tornadoes, snow storms, forest fires, etc. And yes, while we do have hurricanes and some can be really bad, we don't see them as much as those other enviromental issues. Getting past that, we have an amazing amount of positives to offset them.

Our beaches are some of the best in the United States, if not the world. We have white sandy beaches along the Emerald Coast which stretches along the panhandle and northern part of the Gulf of America. Then we have the beaches along the Atlantic Ocean side favored because of many of the popular "party" cities. Then we have the Florida Keys beaches.

Florida also has a surprising amount of natural beauty away from the beaches. Its many freshwater springs, rivers, forests, and wildlife provide plenty of opportunities for swimming, kayaking, hiking, and camping.

Besides the natural beauty of Florida, we have many wonderful attractions: Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando, Everglades National Park, NASA Kennedy Space Center, Busch Gardens, Sea World's Water Park, and many, many others offereing a wide variety of places to enjoy.

A "special" time in Florida is Spring Break, where we are inundated with millions of young people all looking to see who can outdo who in the areas of frivolity, partying, sex, and just plain insanity. Many of whom get to enjoy the wonderful amenities of our local jails for their efforts.

Florida also houses many military bases and some of the largest military ranges in the continental U.S. This is where I fell in love with this state. While stationed down here, I was able to enjoy sandy beaches, fishing, scuba diving, crabbing, and much more! It is a terrific place for me to have retired!
